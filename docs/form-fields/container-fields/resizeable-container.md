# Resizeable Container

Fluent Forms gives you control over **Column Width**. You can drag column borders to resize columns without code, or type exact values in the **Column Width %** setting. The minimum column width is 10%. See [Container](/add-container-fields) for those settings.

This article shows you how to add and resize containers in your form.

## Add a Container Field

1. Open your form in the editor.
2. Drag and drop a container field into the form, or click the **Plus Icon** to add the [Container](/add-container-fields) field.
3. Add the fields you need inside the container, such as **Name**, **Email**, **Radio Button**, or **Custom Submit Button**.

![Resizable Container](/images/form-fields/container-fields/resizeable-container/resizable-container-01-scaled.webp)

Drag the column borders left or right to adjust the width. Use the **Undo** option to revert a change.

When the layout looks right, save your settings. The container keeps proper spacing for the column width you select.

![Resizeable Container step](/images/form-fields/container-fields/resizeable-container/gif.gif)

## Embed the Form into Frontend

Click **Save** to keep your changes. Click the **Preview** (eye) icon at the top right to see the form preview.

To show the form on a page or post, copy the **Shortcode** from the top right and paste it into that page or post.

![Resizeable Container step](/images/form-fields/container-fields/resizeable-container/7.webp)
