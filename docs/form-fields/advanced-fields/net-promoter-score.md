# Net Promoter Score

The **Net Promoter Score (NPS)** is a customer **loyalty and satisfaction** measure. It shows how likely customers are to recommend your products or services. It suits [surveys and polls](/surveys-and-polls). You collect the score by asking customers to **rate** their likelihood of recommending a product or service on a **scale from 0 to 10**.

This guide shows you how to add and customize the **Net Promoter Score** field in Fluent Forms.

> [!Note]
> The **Net Promoter Score** field requires **Fluent Forms Pro**. See [Upgrade to Fluent Forms Pro Add-on](/upgrade-to-pro-add-on).

## Adding Net Promoter Score Field

Go to **Forms** in the Fluent Forms navbar. Click the title of the form you want to edit to open the form editor.

> [!Note]
> If you have no forms yet, read [Create a Form from Scratch](/create-a-form) or [Create a Form using Templates](/using-and-customizing-pre-built-quick-forms).

![Editor](/images/form-fields/advanced-fields/net-promoter-score/Editor-Form-01-3-scaled.webp)

In the editor, click the **Plus Icon** and open the **Advanced Fields** module. You can also find **Advanced Fields** in the right sidebar under the **Input Fields** tab.

Click the **Net Promoter Score** field, or drag and drop it into your editor.

![Adding Net Promoter](/images/form-fields/advanced-fields/net-promoter-score/Adding-net-promoter-02-scaled.webp)

## Customizing the Net Promoter Score Field

After you add the **Net Promoter Score** field, hover over it and click the **Pencil Icon**. The **Customize** tab opens on the right with these options:

- Net Promoter Score Options

- Advanced Options

![Customize Fluent Forms](/images/form-fields/advanced-fields/net-promoter-score/input-customization-03-25-scaled.webp)

### Net Promoter Score Input Options

These options in the **Customize** tab configure the **Net Promoter Score** field.

**A. Element Label:** The field title users see when they fill out the **Net Promoter Score** field. Enter text that explains the purpose of the field.

**B. Admin Field Label:** The admin field label is a title shown only to admins. Use it to show a different label when admins view form submissions.

**C. Label Placement:** Set where the label appears. The options are **Default**, **Top**, **Right**, **Bottom**, **Left**, and **Hide Label**. **Default** uses your global label placement settings.

**D. Required:** Turn on **Required** to make users fill in this field before they submit.

**E. Promoter Start Text:** Set the starting text for the rating scale.

**F. Promoter End Text:** Set the ending text for the rating scale.

![Net Promoter Score](/images/form-fields/advanced-fields/net-promoter-score/net-promoter-score-04-scaled.webp)

### Advanced Options

Advanced settings let you adapt the field to specific use cases.

**A. Name Attribute:** The input field’s name attribute is the HTML equivalent of the same name. You do not need to change it.

**B. Help Message:** Add text that appears as a help message for the user.

**C. Container Class:** Add custom CSS classes to the field’s wrapper.

**D. Element Class:** Add custom CSS classes to the input field.

**E. Conditional Logic:** Create rules that show or hide the field based on submission values. To learn more, read the [Conditional Logic documentation](/set-up-forms-with-conditional-logic). 

![Advanced Options Fluent Forms](/images/form-fields/advanced-fields/net-promoter-score/advanced-options-05-20-scaled.webp)

## Embed the Form into Front

Click **Save** to keep your changes. Click the **Preview** (eye) icon at the top right to see the form preview.

To show the form on a page or post, copy the **Shortcode** from the top right and paste it into that page or post.
