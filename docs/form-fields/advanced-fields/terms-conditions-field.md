# Terms & Conditions Field

The **Terms & Conditions** field in Fluent Forms lets you collect user consent before users submit the form. For data-collection consent, you can also use the [GDPR Agreement field](/gdpr-agreement-field). When users check the consent box, Fluent Forms records their response along with the Terms & Conditions title and description at the time of submission.

This article shows you how to add the **Terms & Conditions** field to your form.

## Adding Terms & Conditions Field

Go to **Forms** in the Fluent Forms navbar. Click the title of the form you want to edit to open the form editor.

> [!Note]
> If you have no forms yet, read [Create a Form from Scratch](/create-a-form) or [Create a Form using Templates](/using-and-customizing-pre-built-quick-forms).

![Edit Terms & Conditions Field](/images/form-fields/advanced-fields/terms-conditions-field/edit-form-01-13-scaled.webp)

In the editor, click the **Plus Icon** and open the **Advanced Fields** module. You can also find **Advanced Fields** in the right sidebar under the **Input Fields** tab.

Click the **Term & Condition** field, or drag and drop it into your editor.

![Terms & Conditions Field step](/images/form-fields/advanced-fields/terms-conditions-field/new-18-scaled.webp)

## Customizing the Terms & Conditions Field

After you add the Terms & Conditions field, hover over it and click the **Pencil Icon**. The **Customize** tab opens on the right side with these options:

- Terms & Conditions Options

- Advanced Options

![Customize Fluent Forms](/images/form-fields/advanced-fields/terms-conditions-field/input-customization-03-16-scaled.webp)

### Terms & Conditions Options

These options in the **Customize** tab configure the **Terms & Conditions** field.

**A. Admin Field Label:** The admin field label is a title shown only to admins. Use it to show a different label when admins view form submissions.

**B. Required:** Turn on **Required** to make users check this box before they submit.

**C. Terms & Conditions:** Enter the content for the consent checkbox as HTML.

**D. Dynamic Smartcodes:** Display dynamic content using [smart codes](/form-editor-smart-codes).

For example, the shortcode could be {dynamic.YOUR_INPUT_NAME}.

**Show Checkbox:** Check **Show Checkbox** to show the checkbox to form users. Uncheck it to hide the checkbox.

![Basic Options Fluent Forms](/images/form-fields/advanced-fields/terms-conditions-field/Basic-options-04-14-scaled.webp)

### Advanced Options

Advanced settings let you adapt the field to specific use cases.

**A. Container Class:** Add custom CSS classes to the field’s wrapper.

**B. Element Class:** Add custom CSS classes to the input field.

**C. Name Attribute:** The input field’s name attribute is the HTML equivalent of the same name. You do not need to change it.

**D. Conditional Logic:** Create rules that show or hide the field based on submission values. To learn more, read the [Conditional Logic documentation](/set-up-forms-with-conditional-logic). 

![Advanced Options Fluent Forms](/images/form-fields/advanced-fields/terms-conditions-field/Advanced-options-05-12-scaled.webp)

### Embed the Form into the Front

Click **Save** to apply your changes.

To show the form on a page or post, copy the **Shortcode** from the top-right corner and paste it into that page or post.

To preview the form, click the **Preview** (eye) icon at the top right.

![Save Terms & Conditions Field](/images/form-fields/advanced-fields/terms-conditions-field/save-form-06-16-scaled.webp)
