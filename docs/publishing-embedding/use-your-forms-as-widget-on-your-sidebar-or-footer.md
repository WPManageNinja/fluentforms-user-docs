# Use Your Forms as Widget on your Sidebar or Footer

Fluent Forms lets you **embed forms directly into your sidebar or footer** as widgets, with no code.

This guide shows you how to add your form as a widget in your WordPress sidebar or footer.

## Access WordPress Widget Settings

First, [create a form](/create-a-form) to use in the widget.

1. Go to your WordPress Dashboard, hover over **Appearance**, and click **Widgets**.
2. Select the section where you want the form, **Sidebar** or **Footer**.
3. Click the **Plus (+) Icon**. A popup opens.
4. Type **Fluent Forms** in the search box.
5. Click the **Fluent Forms Widget**.

![Widgets Footer Fluent Forms](/images/publishing-embedding/use-your-forms-as-widget-on-your-sidebar-or-footer/Widgets-footer.webp)

## Configure the Widget

Add a **Title** for the widget. Choose the **Form** you want to display from the dropdown. Click **Save** or **Update** to apply the changes.

![Widgets Adhd Wordpress](/images/publishing-embedding/use-your-forms-as-widget-on-your-sidebar-or-footer/Widgets-_-ADHD-_-WordPress.webp)

## Preview the Form

Open your site’s front end. Your form now shows in the **sidebar** or **footer**, ready for visitors.

![Widget Footer Fluent Forms](/images/publishing-embedding/use-your-forms-as-widget-on-your-sidebar-or-footer/Widget-Form-in-Footer.webp)
