# Oxygen Builder Widget

The Oxygen Builder Widget lets you add and style Fluent Forms without code. You can change your form colors, shapes, sizes, backgrounds, margins, padding, and more.

This guide shows you how to customize and publish forms with the Fluent Forms Oxygen Widget.

## Add Fluent Forms with Oxygen Builder Widget

Install and activate the **[Oxygen Builder](https://oxygenbuilder.com/)** plugin on your WordPress site. Create a new page and click **Edit with Oxygen** to open the **Oxygen** editor.

![Add Page With Oxygen Builder Fluent Forms with Oxygen](/images/publishing-embedding/oxygen-builder-widget/Add-page-with-Oxygen-builder-scaled.webp)

Click the **Plus Icon (+)** in the top left and type **Fluent Form** in the search bar. Click the **Fluent Form** block to add it.

![Search Fluent Forms](/images/publishing-embedding/oxygen-builder-widget/search-fluent-form-02.webp)

Choose a form from the dropdown. If you have no forms yet, [create a form](/create-a-form) first.

![Select Fluent Forms with Oxygen](/images/publishing-embedding/oxygen-builder-widget/Select-a-form-04.webp)

## Customizing Form Appearance

### Primary

The **Primary** section styles the main parts of your form.

**Form Container:** Change the background color, set the maximum width, and adjust the padding and margins to control the spacing around your form.

You can also set the border color, width, and style (solid, dashed, dotted, or none). Adjust the border radius to round the corners.

![Container Fluent Forms](/images/publishing-embedding/oxygen-builder-widget/container-scaled.webp)

**Labels:** Use the color picker to choose the text and asterisk colors. Set the typography: font family, size, weight, color, alignment, letter spacing, line height, decoration, and style.

**Input & Textarea:** Style the **Input** and **Textarea** fields. You can adjust the label color, background, width, height, margins, and padding. You can also set the text indent and the border width, color, style, and corner radius.

**File Upload Field:** Style the File Upload button. You can change its color, shape, typography, and border, including font size, weight, alignment, border style, width, and radius.

**Multi-step Forms:** For [multi-step forms](/creating-a-multi-step-form), style a **Section Break** and a **Progress Bar** to divide the form into steps and show users their progress.

![Textera Fluent Forms](/images/publishing-embedding/oxygen-builder-widget/Textera-05-scaled.webp)

**Box Shadow:** Add a shadow around your form for a soft depth effect. Adjust the shadow color, direction, blur, and spread.

![Box Fluent Forms](/images/publishing-embedding/oxygen-builder-widget/Box--scaled.webp)

**Submit Button:** Change the button color, width, margin, padding, and more to fit your form.

![Submit Button](/images/publishing-embedding/oxygen-builder-widget/submit-button-06-2-scaled.webp)

## Advanced

The **Advanced** section lets you add effects such as animations, text shadows, and transitions. You can change the opacity of your form and submit button. You can also use basic filters and transformations.

![Advanced Fluent Forms](/images/publishing-embedding/oxygen-builder-widget/advanced-07.webp)

When you finish designing your form, click **Apply Params**, then click **Save**.

![Apply Fluent Forms](/images/publishing-embedding/oxygen-builder-widget/apply--scaled.webp)

### Publishing Your Form

Publish or update the page to make the form live.
