# Embed Your Forms Using Elementor Widget

Fluent Forms integrates with Elementor, so you can add and design forms on your WordPress pages without code. You can adjust colors, shapes, sizes, backgrounds, margins, padding, and more.

This guide shows you how to add and style a form with the Fluent Forms widget in Elementor.

## Create a Form in Elementor

Open the [Elementor](https://elementor.com/) editor for the page where you want to add your form. Type **Fluent Forms** in the **Search Bar**. Select the **Fluent Forms** widget to add a **block** for your form.

![embed your forms using Elementor Widget step](/images/publishing-embedding/embed-your-forms-using-elementor-widget/01-3.webp)

Choose the form you want from the dropdown menu in the widget. Your existing forms appear there, and you can **Select a Fluent Forms** form. If you have no forms yet, [create a new form](/create-a-form).

![embed your forms using Elementor Widget step](/images/publishing-embedding/embed-your-forms-using-elementor-widget/02-9.webp)

## Customizing Form Style

The Fluent Forms widget lets you style your form to fit your site. The **Style** tab has these settings:

**Form Container:** Change the background, set the form’s width, and adjust its margin and alignment.

- **Background Type:** Choose a solid color or upload an image for the form's background.
- **Color:** Pick a global color or use the color picker for form elements.
- **Image:** Upload or select a background image.
- **Link Color:** Set the color for any links in your form.
- **Max Width:** Set the form’s maximum width.
- **Alignment:** Align the form left, center, or right.
- **Margins:** Add space outside the form (top, right, bottom, left).
- **Padding:** Add space inside the form borders.
- **Border Type:** Choose a border style (solid, double, dashed, dotted, groove, etc.).
- **Border Radius:** Round the form’s corners.
- **Box Shadow:** Add a shadow around the form and adjust its horizontal and vertical offset, blur, and spread.

![embed your forms using Elementor Widget step](/images/publishing-embedding/embed-your-forms-using-elementor-widget/04-5.webp)

**Input & Textarea:** Style the Input and Textarea fields.

- **Background Color:** Change the field background.
- **Text Color:** Pick the text color from the color picker.
- **Border Type:** Choose a border style (solid, dashed, dotted, or none).
- **Border Radius:** Round the corners.

You can also adjust the text indent, field width and height, textarea width and height, padding, spacing, and more.

![embed your forms using Elementor Widget step](/images/publishing-embedding/embed-your-forms-using-elementor-widget/05-1.webp)

**Submit Button:** Style the submit button with these options:

- **Color and Typography:** Change the button’s text and background colors to fit your design.
- **Padding:** Adjust the spacing inside the button.
- **Typography:** Set the font style to match your brand.

![embed your forms using Elementor Widget step](/images/publishing-embedding/embed-your-forms-using-elementor-widget/06.webp)

You can also embed forms in the [Gutenberg block editor](/embed-your-forms-directly-in-gutenberg-layout) or as a sidebar or footer widget.
