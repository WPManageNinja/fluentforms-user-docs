# Embed Your Forms Directly in Gutenberg Layout

Fluent Forms lets you embed your forms directly into the Gutenberg editor, so you can add forms to your pages or posts without leaving the editor.

This guide shows you how to add a Fluent Forms block, style it, and publish it.

## Adding Gutenberg Editor

Open the **Page** or **Post** where you want the form in the **Gutenberg editor**.

Click the **Plus (+) Icon** to add a new block, search for Fluent Forms, and click it.

![Add embed your forms directly](/images/publishing-embedding/embed-your-forms-directly-in-gutenberg-layout/Add-Fluent-Form-scaled.webp)

Select a saved form from the **dropdown menu** in the block. If you have no forms yet, [create a form](/create-a-form) first.

The form appears in the editor preview. Click **Update** or **Publish** to save your changes.

![Select embed your forms directly](/images/publishing-embedding/embed-your-forms-directly-in-gutenberg-layout/select-a-form.webp)

This is the preview of a form embedded with the Gutenberg block.

> You can also add the form by pasting the form [ShortCode](/shortcodes-overview) into the Gutenberg ShortCode block or HTML block.

![Embedded Success Fluent Forms](/images/publishing-embedding/embed-your-forms-directly-in-gutenberg-layout/Form-Embeded-success.webp)

## Customize Form Styling

Select the Fluent Forms block. The **Block Settings** sidebar on the right shows three tabs: **General**, **Misc**, and **Advanced** (the standard WordPress panel). These tabs control your form's design.

### General Tab

This tab styles the individual fields and elements in your form.

**Form Style Template:** Apply a pre-built design by choosing a template: Modern, Classic, Bootstrap style, Inherit style, or Custom (for advanced customization).

**Label Styles:** Change the color and typography for your field labels.

**Input & Textarea:** Set the text color, background color, typography, spacing, and borders for all text fields. You can set different styles for **Normal** and **Focus** (when a user clicks into the field).

**Placeholder Styles:** Set a custom color for the placeholder text in your fields.

**Radio & Checkbox Styles:** Adjust the size and color of radio buttons and checkboxes.

**Button Styles:** Style the submit button. You can set its **Alignment**, **Width**, and styles for the **Normal** and **Hover** states, including:

- Text Color
- Background Color
- Typography
- Padding & Margin
- Box Shadow
- Border

![General Tab Fluent Forms](/images/publishing-embedding/embed-your-forms-directly-in-gutenberg-layout/General-tab.webp)

### Misc Tab

**Container Styles:**

- **Background Type:** Set a Classic (solid color or background image) or a Gradient background for the entire form.
- **Padding & Margin:** Add space inside (padding) or outside (margin) the form container to position it.
- **Box Shadow:** Add a shadow to the form container so it stands out from the page.
- **Form Border Settings:** Add and style a border around the entire form.
