# Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common questions about **Fluent Forms**. If you can't find what you're looking for, see [How to Get Support](/docs/how-to-get-support).

## General

### Is Fluent Forms free?

Yes. The core plugin is free and available on the WordPress plugin directory. The **Pro Add-on** unlocks premium fields, integrations, payments, conversational forms, and more.

### Do I need to install Fluent Forms before installing the Pro Add-on?

Yes. The Pro Add-on requires the free core plugin to be installed and activated first. See [How to Install Fluent Forms](/docs/how-to-install-fluent-forms).

### Can I migrate from Contact Form 7 / WPForms / Gravity Forms?

Yes. Fluent Forms has built-in importers for Contact Form 7, WPForms, Gravity Forms, Ninja Forms, and more. See [Fluent Forms Migrator](/docs/fluent-forms-migrator-wpforms-contact-form-7-gravity-forms-ninja-forms-caldera-forms).

### Where is form submission data stored?

All entries are stored in your own WordPress database. Fluent Forms does not send your data to any third-party server.

## Forms & Fields

### How many forms can I create?

Unlimited. There is no cap on forms, fields, or submissions.

### Can I create multi-step forms?

Yes — see [Creating a Multi-Step Form in Fluent Forms](/docs/creating-a-multi-step-form-in-fluent-forms).

### Can I conditionally show or hide fields?

Yes — see [Conditional Logic](/docs/set-up-forms-with-conditional-logic-in-fluent-forms).

## Integrations & Payments

### Which payment gateways are supported?

Stripe, PayPal, Mollie, RazorPay, and Square. See the [Payment Integrations](/docs/how-to-integrate-stripe-with-fluent-forms) section.

### Which email marketing tools are supported?

Mailchimp, ActiveCampaign, ConvertKit, MailerLite, Brevo (Sendinblue), GetResponse, Constant Contact, and 20+ others. See [Email Marketing Integrations](/docs/how-to-integrate-mailchimp-with-fluent-forms).

## Troubleshooting

### My form is not sending email notifications

This is almost always a WordPress mail-delivery issue, not a Fluent Forms issue. See [Fluent Forms Not Sending Email Confirmations](/docs/fluent-forms-not-sending-email-confirmations). We recommend using [FluentSMTP](https://wordpress.org/plugins/fluent-smtp/) for reliable email delivery.

### I'm getting reCAPTCHA / hCaptcha errors

Check that your site keys are correctly configured. See [Spam Protection with Honeypot and Google reCAPTCHA](/docs/spam-protection-with-honeypot-and-google-recaptcha-in-fluent-forms).

### Where can I report a bug?

See [How to Get Support](/docs/how-to-get-support).
