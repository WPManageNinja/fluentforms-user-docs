# Get Support

If you've already gone through the **Docs** and the [Frequently Asked Questions](/frequently-asked-questions) and you're still running into a problem with **Fluent Forms**, you can open a support ticket. This page shows you how to open one and what to include.

## How to Contact Support

Follow these steps to get the fastest and most accurate response.

1. **Visit the WPManageNinja Support Portal:** Go to the [Support Tickets](https://wpmanageninja.com/support-tickets/) page. This is the main channel for opening Fluent Forms support tickets.

2. **Submit a Support Ticket:**

   - Sign in with the account you use on WPManageNinja.com.
   - Open the **Support Tickets** section.
   - Click **Create Ticket**.

3. **Provide Detailed Information:** Share as much context as you can. More detail helps the support team find the cause faster. Include:

   - **A Clear Description of the Problem:** Describe what you're trying to do and what goes wrong instead. (For example: "I'm trying to connect my Mailchimp account to a form, but the integration feed fails to save," or "My payment form submits but Stripe never receives the charge.")
   - **Steps to Reproduce:** List the exact sequence of clicks or actions that triggers the issue.
   - **Screenshots or Screen Recordings:** Include screenshots of your form settings, the integration feed, the entry page, and any visible error on the front end.
   - **Error Messages:** If you see anything in a red box, a browser console error, or a line in your WordPress debug log, copy it into the ticket as text.
   - **Your WordPress Version.**
   - **Your Fluent Forms Free Version** and, if applicable, **your Fluent Forms Pro Add-on Version.**
   - **Your PHP Version** (found under **Tools → Site Health → Info → Server**).
   - **Any Other Active Plugins on Your Site:** List them, especially anything related to email delivery (FluentSMTP, WP Mail SMTP), caching (WP Rocket, LiteSpeed, W3 Total Cache), security (Wordfence, iThemes Security), anti-spam (Akismet, CleanTalk), page builders (Elementor, Oxygen, Beaver Builder, Bricks, Gutenberg blocks), or payment plugins. These are the most common sources of conflict for form submissions, notifications, and payments.

4. **Support Hours:** The support team works during regular business hours and replies to every ticket as soon as possible.

## Other Ways to Get Help

- **Documentation:** Browse the full guides at [docs.fluentforms.com](https://docs.fluentforms.com/).
- **Community:** Ask questions and share ideas in the [WPManageNinja Community](https://community.wpmanageninja.com).
- **Feature Requests:** Suggest a new feature through the [contact page](https://fluentforms.com/contact-us/).
- **Developer Resources:** Find hooks, filters, and the REST API reference at [developers.fluentforms.com](https://developers.fluentforms.com/).

We're committed to helping you get the most out of Fluent Forms!
