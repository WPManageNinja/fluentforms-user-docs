# Get Support

If you've already gone through the **Docs** and the [Frequently Asked Questions](/frequently-asked-questions) and you're still running into a problem with **Fluent Forms**, our support team is ready to help.

## How to Contact Support

To get the fastest and most accurate response, please follow the steps below when you reach out to us:

1. **Visit the WPManageNinja Support Portal:** Head over to [WPManageNinja](https://wpmanageninja.com/account). This is the main channel for opening Fluent Forms support tickets.

2. **Submit a Support Ticket:**

   - Sign in with your account (the same account you use on [WPManageNinja.com](https://wpmanageninja.com/account)).
   - Open the "Support Tickets" section.
   - Click "Create Ticket."

3. **Provide Detailed Information:** When you open the ticket, share as much context as you can. The more we know, the faster we can pinpoint the cause and send you back a fix. Please include:

   - **A Clear Description of the Problem:** Tell us what you're trying to do and what's going wrong instead. (For example: "I'm trying to connect my Mailchimp account to a form, but the integration feed fails to save," or "My payment form submits but Stripe never receives the charge.")
   - **Steps to Reproduce:** Walk us through the exact sequence of clicks or actions that triggers the issue.
   - **Screenshots or Screen Recordings:** A picture is worth a thousand words. Screenshots of your form settings, the integration feed, the entry page, and any visible error on the frontend help us enormously.
   - **Error Messages:** If you see anything in a red box, a browser console error, or a line in your WordPress debug log, copy it into the ticket as text.
   - **Your WordPress Version.**
   - **Your Fluent Forms Free Version** — and, if applicable, **your Fluent Forms Pro Add-on Version.**
   - **Your PHP Version** (found under **Tools → Site Health → Info → Server**).
   - **Any Other Active Plugins on Your Site:** List them, especially anything related to email delivery (FluentSMTP, WP Mail SMTP), caching (WP Rocket, LiteSpeed, W3 Total Cache), security (Wordfence, iThemes Security), anti-spam (Akismet, CleanTalk), page builders (Elementor, Oxygen, Beaver Builder, Bricks, Gutenberg blocks), or payment plugins. These are the most common sources of conflict for form submissions, notifications, and payments.

4. **Support Hours:** Our team works during regular business hours and aims to respond to every ticket as quickly as possible.

We're committed to helping you get the most out of Fluent Forms!
