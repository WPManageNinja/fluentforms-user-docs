# Create a Payment Form

In **Fluent Forms**, you can create **Payment Forms** to accept payments through different gateways. This article shows you how to create a payment form, enable payment methods, and add the form to your site.

## Creating a Payment Form

Go to **Forms** in the **Fluent Forms Navbar**, choose a **Form**, and click the **Pencil/Edit** icon to open its **Editor** page.

> [!Note]
> If you do not have a form yet, read [Create a Form from Scratch](/create-a-form) or [Create a Form using Templates](/using-and-customizing-pre-built-quick-forms) first.

This guide uses an existing form to show the whole process.

![Open Editor Create a Payment Form](/images/payments/getting-started-with-payments/create-a-payment-form/1.-Open-desired-form-Editor-1-scaled.webp)

On the **Editor** page, add the fields you need from the **Payment Fields** module.

![Payment Fields Fluent Forms](/images/payments/getting-started-with-payments/create-a-payment-form/2.-Payment-Fields-scaled.webp)

## Enabling Payment Module

Enable the **Payment Fields** module before you add payment fields to a form.

Click the **Settings** (gear) icon in the **Fluent Forms Navbar**, open **Payment Settings** from the left sidebar, and click **Configuration**. Turn on the **Payment Module** toggle to activate payment-related fields for all forms.

![Enable Payment Module Create a Payment Form](/images/payments/getting-started-with-payments/create-a-payment-form/3.-Enable-payment-module-scaled.webp)

With the **Payment Module** enabled, select the fields your Payment Form needs.

![Payment Item And Payment Method Fields](/images/payments/getting-started-with-payments/create-a-payment-form/4.-Payment-Item-and-Payment-Method-fields-scaled.webp)

## Customizing Payment Method Field

The form now has all the fields a payment form needs.

To edit a field, hover over it and click the **Pencil Icon**. The field's **Customize** tab opens on the right side.

To learn how to edit the **Payment Item** and **Choose Payment Method** fields, see these docs:

- [Payment Item Field](/add-payment-item-field-in-payment-forms)
- [Payment Method Field](/add-payment-method-field-in-payment-forms)

To choose your payment methods, check the platforms you want under **Payment Methods** in the right-side customization menu.

![Editpencil Icon Payment Itel Field](/images/payments/getting-started-with-payments/create-a-payment-form/5.-EditPencil-icon-of-payment-itel-field-scaled.webp)

### Enabling Payment Methods

The **Payment Fields** module lists only the payment methods you have enabled. To enable them for all forms, go to **Settings → Payment Settings → Payment Methods**.

That page lists all the available payment platforms.

Turn on the toggle beside each gateway you want to use, or click **Manage** to open that gateway's settings and finish the setup. Enabled gateways become available in the **Payment Method** field for every form. For gateway setup, see [Accepting Payments](/payments-overview).

![Enable Payment Methods Globally For All Create a Payment Form](/images/payments/getting-started-with-payments/create-a-payment-form/6.-Enable-desired-payment-methods-globally-for-all-forms-scaled.webp)

## Customizing Payment Settings

You can customize payment settings **globally** for all forms or **individually** for each form.

> [!Note]
> To learn how to customize payment settings, see [Configure Payment Settings with Fluent Forms](/configure-payment-settings).

## Integrating Form in WordPress Site

Click **Save** to save your changes. To see a preview, click **Preview** in the form editor.

To display the form on a **Page**/**Post**, copy the **Shortcode** from the top right side.

![Save Payment Create a Payment Form](/images/payments/getting-started-with-payments/create-a-payment-form/9.-Save-Payment-Form-scaled.webp)

Open the Page or Post, paste the **shortcode**, and click **Save**.

![Paste Shortcode On Dsired Page](/images/payments/getting-started-with-payments/create-a-payment-form/10.-Paste-shortcode-on-dsired-page-scaled.webp)

### Preview of Payment Form

The preview below shows the **Payment Form** you created.

![Preview Payment Create a Payment Form](/images/payments/getting-started-with-payments/create-a-payment-form/11.-Preview-of-Payment-form.webp)

Before you go live, configure your gateway in [Payment Settings](/configure-payment-settings), then submit a test payment and check it in your entries.
