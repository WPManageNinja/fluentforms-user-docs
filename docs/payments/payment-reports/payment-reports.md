# Payment Reports

The **Payments** report is a dashboard that tracks all financial transactions processed through your forms. Use it to monitor your revenue, view transaction statuses, and see which forms generate the most income.

## Accessing the Payment Reports

In your WordPress dashboard, go to **Fluent Forms** > **Reports** in the top menu bar. The **Overview** tab opens first. For a tour of the full analytics area, see the [Reports Dashboard Overview](/reports-dashboard-overview).

Select the **Payments** tab to see the payment-related reports. The **Payments** tab appears only when the **Payment Module** is enabled and your user role can view payments.

### Filtering Payment Data

Filters at the top of the page refine the data on the entire dashboard.

- **All Forms:** View payment data for all forms, or select a specific one.
- **Date Range:** Select a time period, such as Last 3 months, or choose a custom date range.

### Payments

The summary cards at the top give an at-a-glance overview of your financial activity for the selected period.

- **Total Paid:** The total amount of completed payments.
- **Total Pending:** The total amount of payments that are initiated but not yet complete.
- **Total Refunded:** The total amount you have refunded to customers.
- **Total Revenue:** The net income after refunds and other adjustments.

## Analyzing Payment Charts and Tables

The dashboard has several sections that show your payment data in detail.

**Payments Chart:** A bar chart of your payment trends over time. Use it to compare the amounts for **Paid**, **Pending**, **Refunded**, and **Total Revenue** statuses on specific dates.

![Payment Charts](/images/payments/payment-reports/payment-reports/Payment-charts-2-scaled.webp)

**Subscription (Pro):** Tracks recurring subscription data collected through the [Subscription](/add-subscription-field-in-payment-forms) field over time. When no subscription payments exist in the selected period, it shows "No subscription data available".

**Transaction Summary:** A snapshot of all transactions. Use the **Recurring** and **One-Time** toggles to switch between transaction types. The chart and list show a breakdown by status, including **Paid**, **Pending**, **Refunded**, **Revenue**, **Cancelled**, and **Failed**.

![Subscription And Transaction](/images/payments/payment-reports/payment-reports/subscription-and-transaction-03-scaled.webp)

**Net Revenue Analysis (Forms) (Pro):** Breaks down your revenue by form. The columns show the **Paid**, **Pending**, **Refunded**, and **Net Revenue** amounts for each form, so you can identify your most profitable ones.

Use the dropdown in the top-right corner to analyze payments by **Payment Method** and **Payment Type**.

![Payment Analysis By](/images/payments/payment-reports/payment-reports/payment-analysis-by-form-04-scaled.webp)

**Net Revenue Analysis (Payment Method):** Shows which payment gateways your customers use. It breaks down all financial data by **Payment Method**, such as **PayPal** or **Offline/Test**.

Use it to compare the **Paid**, **Pending**, and **Net Revenue** figures for each payment processor you have enabled. The **Count** column shows the number of transactions each method processed.

![Payment Method](/images/payments/payment-reports/payment-reports/payment-method-05-scaled.webp)

**Net Revenue Analysis (Payment Type):** Groups your transaction data by payment model, such as **One-time Payment**.

The columns show the total **Paid**, **Pending**, **Refunded**, and **Net Revenue** amounts, plus a **Count** of total transactions for that type.

![Payment Type](/images/payments/payment-reports/payment-reports/payment-type-06.webp)
