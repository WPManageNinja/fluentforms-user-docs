# Accepting Payments

Turn any form into a checkout. **Fluent Forms** lets you collect one-time payments, subscriptions, and donations right inside your forms — no separate cart or extra plugin required. Add payment fields, connect your favorite gateway, and watch the revenue roll in while you track every transaction in one place. This page walks you through the whole payment journey.

## Getting Started with Payments

Lay the foundation. Create your first payment form and configure how money is collected.

- [Create a Payment Form](/how-to-create-a-payment-form-with-fluent-form) — build a form that accepts payments from scratch.
- [Payment Settings](/configure-payment-settings-with-fluent-forms) — set your currency, gateways, and global payment options.

## Payment Fields

Build flexible pricing. Mix and match fields to charge fixed prices, custom amounts, quantities, and recurring plans.

- [Payment Item](/add-payment-item-field-in-payment-forms) — sell products or services at set prices.
- [Custom Amount](/add-custom-payment-amount-field-in-payment-forms) — let users enter their own amount, great for donations.
- [Item Quantity](/add-item-quantity-field-in-payment-forms) — let buyers choose how many they want.
- [Subscription](/add-subscription-field-in-payment-forms) — charge recurring payments on a schedule.
- [Coupon](/add-coupon-field-in-payment-forms) — offer discounts with coupon codes.
- [Payment Method](/add-payment-method-field-in-payment-forms) — let users pick how they pay.
- [Payment Summary](/add-payment-summary-field-in-payment-forms) — show a clear breakdown before checkout.
