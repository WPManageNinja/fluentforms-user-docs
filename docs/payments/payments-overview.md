# Accepting Payments

Turn any form into a checkout. **Fluent Forms** lets you collect one-time payments, subscriptions, and donations inside your forms, with no separate cart or extra plugin. Add payment fields, connect a payment gateway, and track every transaction in one place. This page walks you through the payment journey.

## Getting Started with Payments

Create your first payment form and configure how money is collected.

- [Create a Payment Form](/create-a-payment-form) — build a form that accepts payments from scratch.
- [Payment Settings](/configure-payment-settings) — set your currency, gateways, and global payment options. Configure these before you test a payment form.

## Payment Fields

Mix and match fields to charge fixed prices, custom amounts, quantities, and recurring plans.

- [Payment Item](/add-payment-item-field-in-payment-forms) — sell products or services at set prices.
- [Custom Amount](/add-custom-payment-amount-field-in-payment-forms) — let users enter their own amount, for example for donations.
- [Item Quantity](/add-item-quantity-field-in-payment-forms) — let buyers choose how many they want.
- [Subscription](/add-subscription-field-in-payment-forms) — charge recurring payments on a schedule.
- [Coupon](/add-coupon-field-in-payment-forms) (Pro) — offer discounts with coupon codes.
- [Payment Method](/add-payment-method-field-in-payment-forms) — let users pick how they pay.
- [Payment Summary](/add-payment-summary-field-in-payment-forms) — show a breakdown before checkout.

## Payment Gateways

Connect the gateway you want to use. Stripe is available in the free plugin. The other gateways need Fluent Forms Pro.

- [Stripe](/integrate-stripe) — accept cards and wallets with Stripe.
- [PayPal](/integrate-paypal) (Pro) — take payments through PayPal.
- [Mollie](/integrate-mollie) (Pro) — collect European payment methods with Mollie.
- [Razorpay](/integrate-razorpay) (Pro) — accept payments in India with Razorpay.
- [Square](/integrate-square-inline-payment-integration) (Pro) — take inline payments with Square.
- [Paddle](/integrate-paddle) (Pro) — sell digital products with Paddle.
- [Paystack](/integrate-paystack) (Pro) — accept payments across Africa with Paystack.
- [Authorize.Net](/integrate-authorizenet) (Pro) — process cards with Authorize.Net.
