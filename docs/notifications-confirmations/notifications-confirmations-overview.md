# Notifications & Confirmations

What happens right after someone hits submit matters just as much as the form itself. **Fluent Forms** lets you email the right people instantly and show users a clear confirmation, so no lead slips through and every visitor knows their message arrived. This page covers both sides: the emails your team receives and the messages your users see.

## Email Notifications

Send tailored emails to admins, team members, or the user as soon as a form is submitted.

- [Admin & User Notifications](/setup-adminuser-email-notifications) — send emails to your team and to the person who submitted.
- [Conditional Notifications](/conditional-email-notification) (Pro) — trigger emails only when conditions are met.
- [Conditional Email Routing](/conditional-email-routing) — send entries to different recipients based on answers.
- [Weekly Email Summary](/weekly-email-summary) — get a digest of submissions delivered on schedule.
- [Double Opt-in Emails](/set-up-double-opt-in-emails) (Pro) — confirm subscribers before adding them.
- [Emails Not Sending (Troubleshooting)](/not-sending-email-confirmations) — fix delivery issues.

## Confirmations

Show a custom message or redirect users after a successful submission.

- [Confirmation Message](/setup-form-submission-confirmation-message) — set the message or redirect users see after submitting.
- [Conditional Confirmation](/conditional-confirmation-message) (Pro) — show different confirmations based on user input.
- [Shortcodes in Confirmations](/shortcodes-in-confirmation-settings) — personalize confirmations with submitted data.

## Where to go next

After notifications are running, manage the entries they create:

- [Managing Entries](/managing-entries) — view, search, and act on every submission.
- [Integrate FluentCRM](/fluentcrm-integration) — send submissions straight into your CRM.
