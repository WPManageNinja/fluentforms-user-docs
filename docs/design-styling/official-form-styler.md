# Official Form Styler

Fluent Forms is designed to look great & fit with any WordPress theme. With the built-in **Form Styler**, you can easily create a custom design that perfectly matches your site's branding without coding knowledge. If you need finer control, you can also apply your own [custom CSS](/stylingcustom-css).

> [!Note]
> **Form Styler** requires **Fluent Forms Pro**. See [Upgrade to Fluent Forms Pro Add-on](/upgrade-to-pro-add-on).

This guide walks you through how to **design, style,** and **customize** your forms visually in Fluent Forms.

## Getting Started with Form Styler

First, open or create a form in Fluent Forms. **Save** the form, then click **Appearance** in the top bar to open the styling panel. The live preview of your form sits on the left, and the style options sit on the right.

![Official Style](/images/design-styling/official-form-styler/official-form-style-01-scaled.webp)

## Styling Options

The styling panel has three tabs: **Elements**, **Message Styling**, and **Import**.

### Elements Tab

The **Elements** tab styles each part of the form. Here you can choose a pre-defined look from **Style Preset**. 

Choose from **Default**, **Modern (Bold)**, **Modern (Light)**, **Classic**, **Bootstrap Style**, **Clear Blue**, **Forest**, **Plum**, **Night**, **Reef**, **Spark**, or **Inherit Theme Style**.

![Official Form Styler of Fluent Forms step](/images/design-styling/official-form-styler/02-6-scaled.webp)

You can also customize the look by choosing **Custom (Advanced Customization)**. This unlocks the **Label Style**, **Placeholder**, **Inputs & Text Area**, **Radio & Checkbox**, **Submit Button**, and **Container** sections below the presets. Use **Reset** to return to the preset defaults, or **Export** to download your style so you can reuse it on another form.

**Label Style:** Open **Label Style** to change the color of the field labels. As shown in the screenshot below, you may also define typography, such as font size, font weights, text decoration, line height, and letter spacing.

![General Label](/images/design-styling/official-form-styler/general-label-01-scaled.webp)

**Inputs & Text Area:** Open **Inputs & Text Area** to set the input fields and text areas with background and text colors. Change the font size, weight, text decoration, line height, letter spacing, etc. The box shadow of the input and text-area boxes can be defined below the **Typography** settings.

![Text Area](/images/design-styling/official-form-styler/Text-area-1-scaled.webp)

Switch between the **Normal** and **Focus** tabs to style each state. In **Focus**, you can add style to the input fields and text regions. This is the same as the standard styling options.

![Input Textarea Fluent Forms](/images/design-styling/official-form-styler/Input-Textarea--scaled.webp)

**Placeholder:** Open **Placeholder** to choose the color of the input field placeholders. Set the typography as indicated in the screenshot, which includes **Font Size, Font Weights, Text Decoration, Line Height, and Letter Spacing**, among other things.

![Placeholder Fluent Forms](/images/design-styling/official-form-styler/Placeholder.webp)

**Radio & Checkbox:** Open **Radio & Checkbox**. When you enable **Smart UI**, you’ll see three styling options for the radio and checkbox fields. When the radio or checkbox is checked, define the border, item, and background colours.

You can also style your form with **Custom Border** by checking the **Use Custom Border Style**.

![Genral Tab Customize Style](/images/design-styling/official-form-styler/Genral-tab-customize-style-1-scaled.webp)

**Image or File Button Style:** Fluent Forms offers the Image or File button Style options. You can customize the button to match your website’s design.

You can personalize the button’s background color, text color, typography, border options, and even add box shadows. 

These customizations can be applied to both the **Normal** state and the **Hover** state.

![Or File Button Style](/images/design-styling/official-form-styler/image-or-file-button-style-06-scaled.webp)

**Submit Button:** Fluent Forms sets the default Submit button color to a vibrant dodger blue. However, you’re not restricted to this; you can easily customize the Submit button to suit your preferences and match your website's design.

The styling options for the **Submit button** are the same as those for the **Next** and **Previous** buttons in [multi-page forms](/creating-a-multi-step-form). 

You can personalize the button’s background color, text color, typography, border options, and even add box shadows. 

These customizations can be applied to both the **Normal** state and the **Hover** state.

![Button Style Official Form Styler of](/images/design-styling/official-form-styler/Button-Style.webp)

### Container

The **Container** section styles the form container background, padding, and margin.

**Container Style:** In the container style options, you can choose various design options, including background, borders, padding, margin, box shadow, etc.

**Form Border Settings:** Enable form border settings and specify the border type, color, width, and radius of the border.

![Official Form Styler of Fluent Forms step](/images/design-styling/official-form-styler/04-5-scaled.webp)

## Message Styling Tab

The **Message Styling** tab styles the messages your form shows, such as the required asterisk, inline errors, and the messages that appear after a submission. Expand a group to edit it.

**Asterisk Styles:** You can choose the color of the asterisk styles of your form here.

![Official Form Styler of Fluent Forms step](/images/design-styling/official-form-styler/05-1-scaled.webp)

**Inline Error Message Styles:** The color of the Inline Error Message is customizable. As shown in the screenshot below, define the typography, including font size, font weights, text decoration, line height, and letter spacing, among other things.

![Official Form Styler of Fluent Forms step](/images/design-styling/official-form-styler/06-scaled.webp)

**After Submit Success Message Styles:** You can also easily customize the After Submit Success Message. Fill out your form and submit it for visual viewing while designing the submit success message for testing purposes.

You can customize the background and fonts. You can change the **Font Size** and weight, **Text Decoration**, **Line Height**, and **Letter Spacing**, among other things. 

The box shadow of the success message box can be defined under the typography settings.

![Official Form Styler of Fluent Forms step](/images/design-styling/official-form-styler/07-scaled.webp)

**After Submit Error Message Styles:** The Error Message can also be customized after submission. Its styles are the same as the After Submit Success Message Styles.

You can set the background and font colours, as well as the font size, weight, text decoration, line height, and letter spacing.

You may also configure the box shadow of the success message box and also add a **Custom Border** in your form.

![Official Form Styler of Fluent Forms step](/images/design-styling/official-form-styler/08-scaled.webp)

## Import Tab

The **Import** tab selects an existing Fluent Forms style and can also import your style.

You can import your style from another **Existing Form** or **Upload** your Fluent form style.

![Official Form Styler of Fluent Forms step](/images/design-styling/official-form-styler/09.webp)
