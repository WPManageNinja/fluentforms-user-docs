# How to Create a Form with Fluent Forms AI

Fluent Forms now integrates Fluent Forms AI, making it easier for users to create forms effortlessly with simple commands. This article guides you through creating a form using **Fluent Forms AI**.

> [!Note]
> **Fluent Forms AI** is the built-in, free AI assistant for quick form creation—no extra setup required. For advanced generation with your own OpenAI API key, use **OpenAI ChatGPT** (Pro). See [How to Create a Form with OpenAI ChatGPT](/docs/how-to-create-a-form-with-openai-chatgpt).

### Create a form with AI

To start, log in to your **WordPress Dashboard**. Navigate to **Fluent Forms** and click **New Form** from the left sidebar. Or open the **Fluent Forms** navbar, click **Forms**, then click **+ Add New Form**.

![Create a form with AI](/images/form-editor/how-to-create-a-form-with-fluent-forms-ai/1.-Add-new-form-button-scaled-2.webp)

A pop-up will appear with several options. Click the **Create using AI** tab.

![Create using AI](/images/form-editor/how-to-create-a-form-with-fluent-forms-ai/2.-Create-Using-AI-tab.webp)

A new pop-up opens with the following options:

* **Create a Form for:** Enter the title and purpose as prompts to specify the form you want to create (for example, survey, payment, or registration form).
* **Suggestions:** Pre-built commands you can select. Click a suggestion and the ready-made command is added to the **Create a Form for** field automatically.
* **Including these questions:** Optional extra instructions for the fields or information you want on the form.

**For example**, to collect product reviews, choose the **Product Reviews** suggestion and add any extra instructions you need.

When you finish, click **Create**.

![Create](/images/form-editor/how-to-create-a-form-with-fluent-forms-ai/3.-Create-button.webp)

Fluent Forms AI builds your form from your prompts—for example, a **Product Review Form** ready to edit.

* **Form name:** Click the **Pencil** icon in the top-left corner to rename the form.

![Form Name](/images/form-editor/how-to-create-a-form-with-fluent-forms-ai/4.-Form-is-ready-scaled.webp)

## Form Customization

On the editor page, customize each field after AI generates your form. Hover over a field to see these options:

* **Drag & Drop:** Move fields anywhere on the form canvas.
* **Move Up:** Move the selected field one position up.
* **Move Down:** Move the selected field one position down.
* **Edit:** Click the **Pencil** icon to open **Input Customization** in the right sidebar.
* **Duplicate:** Copy the selected field.
* **Delete:** Click the **Trash** icon to remove the field.

> [!Note]
> Each field has a dedicated guide under **General Fields**, **Advanced Fields**, **Payment Fields**, and related sections in the documentation sidebar. For Form Builder layout and editor options, see [Fluent Forms User Interface](/docs/fluent-forms-user-interface) and [How to Create a Form with Fluent Forms](/docs/how-to-create-a-form-with-fluent-forms).

![Form Customization](/images/form-editor/how-to-create-a-form-with-fluent-forms-ai/5.-Input-Customization-scaled.webp)

## Add New Fields

From the editor, add more fields as needed:

* **Plus icon:** Click **+**, open the field group you need, and select a field.
* **Input Fields tab:** On the right, expand a field section with the dropdown arrow and choose a field.
* **Search bar:** Type a field name to find it quickly.

> **Tip:** See the **General Fields**, **Advanced Fields**, and other field categories in the documentation sidebar for guides on each input type.

![Add New Fields](/images/form-editor/how-to-create-a-form-with-fluent-forms-ai/6.-Add-new-fields-scaled.webp)

## Embed Form into Frontend

When your form is ready:

* **Shortcode:** Copy the shortcode from the top-right corner and paste it into any page, post, or widget area.
* **Preview & Design:** Click **Preview & Design** to see how the form looks on the front end.
* **Save Form:** Click **Save Form** to store your changes.

![Embed Form](/images/form-editor/how-to-create-a-form-with-fluent-forms-ai/7.-Save-Form-scaled.webp)
