# Configuring Your Forms

A great form does more than collect data — it guides people, validates input, and adapts to every answer. **Fluent Forms** gives you fine-grained control over how each form behaves, from custom messages and access rules to powerful conditional logic and live calculations. This page shows you everything you can configure and links you to the right guide.

## Form Settings

Set the rules and messaging that shape the submission experience.

- [Error Messages](/error-message-customization-with-fluent-forms) — customize the validation messages users see.
- [Help Messages](/help-message-customization-in-fluent-forms) — add helpful hints to any field.
- [Restrict Blank Submission](/restrict-blank-form-submission-with-fluent-forms) — stop empty forms from being submitted.
- [Require User Login](/user-login-requirement-in-fluent-forms) — limit a form to logged-in users.
- [Form Restrictions](/form-restrictions-feature-in-fluent-forms) — cap entries, set limits, and control access.
- [Form Scheduling](/form-scheduling-feature-in-fluent-forms) — open and close a form on a schedule.
- [Keyboard Navigation & Shortcuts](/keyboard-navigation-shortcuts-in-fluent-forms) — let users move through forms faster.
