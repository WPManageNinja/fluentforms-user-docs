# Managing Submissions

Every submission is a lead, a sale, or a story worth acting on. **Fluent Forms** keeps all your entries in one dashboard, where you can search them, edit them, visualize them, and turn the raw data into clear reports. This page lists what you can do with your submissions and links to each guide.

## Entries

View, search, edit, and automate how entries are handled.

- [Managing Entries](/managing-entries) — view and work with all your form submissions.
- [Search & Filter Entries](/search-and-filter-form-entries) — find exactly the entries you need.
- [Import Entries](/importing-entries) — bring entries into a form from a file.
- [Frontend Entry View](/frontend-entry-view) — let users view entries from the front end.
- [Edit Submitted Entries](/edit-user-submitted-entries) (Pro) — correct or update submissions after the fact.
- [Date & Time Stamp](/add-a-date-and-time-stamp-to-form-entries) — record when each entry was submitted.
- [Partial Entries](/partial-entries-for-step-forms) (Pro) — capture answers even when a form isn't finished.
- [Auto-Delete Entries](/automatically-delete-form-entries) — clean up old data automatically.
- [Entry Visualizations](/visual-representation-of-form-entries) — see entries as charts and graphs.
- [Form Edit History](/edit-history-feature) — restore earlier saved versions of a form.
- [Admin Approval](/admin-approval-feature) (Pro) — review and approve entries before feeds and emails run.
- [Show Entries with Ninja Tables](/show-entries-in-frontend-using-ninja-tables) — display entries on the front end with Ninja Tables.

## Reports

Turn data into insight and see how your forms perform.

- [Reports Dashboard](/reports-dashboard-overview) — see key submission metrics at a glance.
- [Submission Reports](/submission-reports) — analyze responses and trends over time.
