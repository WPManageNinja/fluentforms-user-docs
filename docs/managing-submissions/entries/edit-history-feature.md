# Edit History Feature

The **Edit History** feature lets you view the complete history of your form edits and track every change. You can also restore previous versions of your form. For a broader log of actions across your site, see [Activity Logs](/activity-logs).

This article shows you how to use **Edit History**. It tracks changes to the form in the form editor, not changes to submitted entries. To edit a submitted entry, see [Edit User Submitted Entries](/edit-user-submitted-entries).

## Access the History Feature

Go to the **Forms** section in the [Fluent Forms dashboard](/user-interface), then click the form you want to modify.

![Edit Section Fluent Forms](/images/managing-submissions/entries/edit-history-feature/Form-edit-section-scaled.webp)

In the form editor, find the **History** (clock) icon at the top right of the right panel. Click it to open the **Saved versions** list.

![History Section](/images/managing-submissions/entries/edit-history-feature/History-section-1-scaled.webp)

The list shows all your previous form changes. To view or restore a change, hover over the activity and click **Restore**.

To clear your form's edit history, click the **Delete** icon in the top-right corner of the History section. Then click **Save Form** to save your changes.
