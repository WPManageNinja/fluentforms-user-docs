# Add a Date and Time Stamp to Form Entries

Fluent Forms tracks when each form is submitted. A date and time stamp helps you analyze user activity and submission patterns.

This guide shows you how to check the default timestamp and add a custom date and time stamp to form entries.

## Check Default Timestamps

Open the [Entries](/managing-entries) section in your Fluent Forms dashboard. Each submission shows a **Time** column with when the form was submitted, such as "4 minutes ago". The single entry page shows **Date Submitted** and **Submitted On**.

Hover over the timestamp to see the exact **month**, **day**, and **time**.

![Defualt Date And Time](/images/managing-submissions/entries/add-a-date-and-time-stamp-to-form-entries/defualt-date-and-time-01-scaled.webp)

## Add Custom Date and Time Stamps

To add a custom timestamp, open the form editor and add a [Hidden Field](/hidden-input-field) from the **Advanced Fields** section.

![Hidden Field](/images/managing-submissions/entries/add-a-date-and-time-stamp-to-form-entries/Hidden-field-02-scaled.webp)

Click the **three-dot menu** in the **Input Customization** section. Select one of the predefined formats:

- **Date (mm/dd/yyyy)**
- **Date (dd/mm/yyyy)**

Then click **Save Form**.

![a Date and Time Stamp to Form Entries step](/images/managing-submissions/entries/add-a-date-and-time-stamp-to-form-entries/03-1-scaled.webp)

When a user submits the form, the custom date and time stamp appears in the **Entries** section.

![a Date and Time Stamp to Form Entries step](/images/managing-submissions/entries/add-a-date-and-time-stamp-to-form-entries/04-scaled.webp)
