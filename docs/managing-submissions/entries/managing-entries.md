# Managing Entries

Fluent Forms allows you to track, view, and manage all your form submissions without opening each form individually. Every user submission is automatically saved in the backend, and you can easily see a summary of all submissions from the **All Forms** screen.

In this article, we'll show you how to manage, view, and export entries in Fluent Forms.

## Entries Dashboards

To access the Entries Dashboard, go to the **Fluent Forms Dashboard** and click on the **Entries** tab from the top menu. Here you will find all your form entries in one place.

You will see all the global entries here, with summary cards for **Total Entries**, **Unread Entries**, and **Read Entries**. Use the **All Forms** dropdown to filter entries by form. Use the tabs above the table (**All**, **Unread**, **Read**, **Favorites**, **Spam**, and **Trashed**) to organize your submissions.

Click the **Search** icon in the table toolbar to quickly find specific form entries.

If you want to bring in entries from another source, go to **Tools** > **Import Entries**. To learn more, check out [Importing Entries in Fluent Forms](/importing-entries).

The Entries Dashboard makes it simple to search, filter, and manage your form submissions, all from one place.

![Entries Dashboards Fluent Forms](/images/managing-submissions/entries/managing-entries/entries-dashboards-scaled.webp)

### Show Chart

The **Entries** **Dashboard** shows a bar chart of your entries by date. Use the **Show Chart** toggle at the top to display or hide it. For deeper, field-level charts, see [Visual Representation of Form Entries](/visual-representation-of-form-entries).

Turn the **Show Chart** toggle off to remove the chart view anytime.

![Show Chart Fluent Forms](/images/managing-submissions/entries/managing-entries/show-chart-scaled.webp)

### Filter

You will find a **Date Range** field in the top right corner of the entries dashboard.

Click it to filter your submissions by **Today**, **Last week**, **Last month**, or **Last 3 months**. You can also pick a start date and an end date on the calendar to view entries from a specific period.

![Entries Filter Fluent Forms](/images/managing-submissions/entries/managing-entries/Entries-filter-scaled.webp)

You can adjust the **per page** option in the pagination at the bottom of the page to show more entries at once.

![Pagination Fluent Forms](/images/managing-submissions/entries/managing-entries/Pagination-scaled.webp)

## Managing Entries

Fluent Forms offers a powerful entry management system for handling form submissions. With submitted entries, you can manage, highlight, edit, and process them individually or in bulk.

**Status tabs:** The tabs above the table show **All**, **Unread**, **Read**, **Favorites**, **Spam**, and **Trashed** entries. Click a tab to sort your entries quickly.

**Advanced Filter:** If you need more specific filtering, turn on the **Advanced Filter** by clicking the toggle button. For a focused walkthrough, see [How to Search and Filter Form Entries](/search-and-filter-form-entries). Then click the **Add** button to set your filter options. You can filter entries by **Inputs**, **User**, and **Entry Attributes**. Click **Filter** to apply them.

Conditions inside a group are joined with AND. Click **Add OR Group** to add another group of conditions. To remove a filter, click the **Delete** icon, or click **Clear Filters** to **clear** **all filters** at once.

![Advanced Filter](/images/managing-submissions/entries/managing-entries/advanced-filter-01-scaled.webp)

**Search:** Click the **Search** icon and type a **name**, **email**, or **submission ID** to quickly find entries.

**Columns:** Click the **Columns** icon to choose which columns you want to display.

**Date Range:** Filter your entries by selecting a specific date or date range at the top of the page.

To view the details of an entry, click its ID. To act on a single entry, click the **three-dot** menu at the end of the row. To mark an entry as a favorite, click its **star** icon.

![Entries Advanced Filter](/images/managing-submissions/entries/managing-entries/entries-advanced-filter-scaled.webp)

After opening an entry, you will be taken to the detailed page of the selected entry, where you can see all the information at a glance.

**Form Entry Data:** Here you’ll find the main entry details like the **Name** and **Email** of the person who submitted the form.

**Submission Notes:** You can add notes related to the submission by clicking the **Add Note** button. It's a great way to keep track of important information or follow-up actions.

**Submission Logs:** All the logs related to the submission are displayed here, with **General** and **API Calls** tabs, helping you track any updates or changes made over time.

**Submission Info:** This section shows all the detailed information about the submission. You can edit the entry by clicking the **Edit** icon. For details, see [Edit User Submitted Entries With Fluent Forms](/edit-user-submitted-entries).

If needed, you can also change the submission status using the **Change Status to** dropdown.

**Print Entries:** To print individual form submissions, click the **Print** icon at the top right of the page. Use the **arrow** buttons next to it to move to the previous or next entry.

![Unnamed Fluent Forms](/images/managing-submissions/entries/managing-entries/unnamed-4.webp)

### Export Entries

Fluent Forms makes exporting your form entries quick and easy. You can export entries individually or in bulk, and choose from different file formats like **CSV**, **XLSX**, **ODS**, or **JSON**.

Whether you want to export all entries, only favorites, or just unread submissions, Fluent Forms gives you full control for easy data analysis or sharing.

To export from a form's **Entries** page, click the **Export** button at the top, then choose **Export as CSV**, **Export as Excel (xlsx)**, **Export as ODS**, or **Export as JSON Data**.

![Unnamed Fluent Forms](/images/managing-submissions/entries/managing-entries/unnamed-5.webp)

You can also choose which fields to include in the export from **Tools** > **Export Entries**. If you don't select any fields, Fluent Forms exports the default entry columns instead of every field in the form.

Now, you can easily organize, manage, and process all your submitted entries in Fluent Forms.
