# Visual Representation of Form Entries

Fluent Forms shows form entries as **Pie**, **Bar**, and **Horizontal bar** charts, so you can analyze and print reports. For site-wide submission volume and status trends, see [Submission Reports](/submission-reports).

This guide shows you how to get a visual representation of form entries.

## Access the Entries

There are two types of entries:

- **All Entries:** A global view of all entries submitted across all forms.
- **Individual Form Entries:** Entries submitted to a specific form.

You can open the entries in three ways.

First, go to the Fluent Forms **Forms** section and click the **Entries** icon on your form's row.

![Visual Entries Fluent Forms](/images/managing-submissions/entries/visual-representation-of-form-entries/visual-form-entries-scaled.webp)

Second, open your form and select **Entries** from the top bar.

![Entries Fluent Forms](/images/managing-submissions/entries/visual-representation-of-form-entries/entries-02-scaled.webp)

Third, go to **Fluent Forms Entries** and select a form from the **All Forms** dropdown.

![Entries Fluent Forms](/images/managing-submissions/entries/visual-representation-of-form-entries/entries-03-scaled.webp)

## View the Visual Report

On the form's **Entries** page, click the **three-dot** menu in the top-right corner and choose **Visual Report**. This opens the **Visual Data Reporting** page for the form.

![Visual Representation of Form Entries step](/images/managing-submissions/entries/visual-representation-of-form-entries/111-04-10-2025_10_12_AM.webp)

The page shows visual data for choice fields such as **checkboxes**, **dropdowns**, and **radio** fields, plus ratings, net promoter score, country select, and tabular grid fields. With Pro, the ranking field shows a rank distribution. The report displays total submissions and the percentage for each selected input.

![Chart Fluent Forms](/images/managing-submissions/entries/visual-representation-of-form-entries/chart-05.webp)

Use the chart icons to switch between **Pie chart**, **Bar chart**, and **Horizontal bar chart**. Click **Print Report** to print the report.

The **Other info** sections show the total number of form entries, the browser used during submission, and the device name. Use the **Filter Data by Status** checkboxes (Unread, Read, Favorites, and Spam) to refine the report. Click **View Regular Entries** to return to the entries table.

![Print Report](/images/managing-submissions/entries/visual-representation-of-form-entries/print-report-06-scaled.webp)

> [!Note]
> Charts are generated only for supported fields: select, radio, checkbox, ratings, net promoter score, select country, and tabular grid fields (and the ranking field with Pro).

Use **Reset Form Analytics** to clear the analytics data for the form.
