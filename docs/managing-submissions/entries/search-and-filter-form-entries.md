# Search and Filter Form Entries

Fluent Forms lets you search and filter form entries to find specific data quickly and [manage your submissions](/managing-entries). This guide shows you how to use these features step by step.

## Search Entries

Go to the **Entries** section of your form. Click the **Search** icon in the table toolbar, then type in the **Search Entry** box. Use keywords such as a name, email, or other detail to find entries.

![Search and Filter Form Entries step](/images/managing-submissions/entries/search-and-filter-form-entries/01-scaled.webp)

## Filter Entries

Fluent Forms offers several filtering options.

### Status Tabs

Click one of the status tabs above the entries table to filter by type:

- **Unread:** Shows entries not yet reviewed.
- **Read:** Shows entries already viewed.
- **Favorites:** Shows entries marked as favorites.
- **Spam:** Shows entries flagged as spam. To set up spam detection, see [Integrate Akismet](/integrate-akismet) and [Spam Protection with Honeypot and reCAPTCHA](/spam-protection-with-honeypot-and-google-recaptcha).
- **Trashed:** Shows deleted entries.

![Search and Filter Form Entries step](/images/managing-submissions/entries/search-and-filter-form-entries/02-scaled.webp)

**Date Range:** Click the **Date Range** field at the top of the page. Choose a pre-defined timeframe (**Today**, **Last week**, **Last month**, or **Last 3 months**), or pick a custom start and end date on the calendar.

![Search and Filter Form Entries step](/images/managing-submissions/entries/search-and-filter-form-entries/03-scaled.webp)

## Advanced Filter

Turn on the **Advanced Filter** toggle, then click **Add** to set your filter options. You can filter entries by **Inputs**, **User**, and **Entry Attributes**. Click **Filter** to apply them.

Conditions inside a group are joined with AND. Click **Add OR Group** to add another group of conditions, or **Duplicate Group** to copy one. To remove a filter, click the **Delete** icon. To clear all filters at once, click **Clear Filters**.

![Advanced Filter](/images/managing-submissions/entries/managing-entries/advanced-filter-01-scaled.webp)

## Payment Status Filter

On forms with payments, use **Filter by payment status** to filter entries by status, such as **Paid**, **Pending**, **Processing**, **Failed**, **Refunded**, or **Cancelled**.
