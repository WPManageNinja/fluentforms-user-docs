# Using and Customizing Pre-built Quick Forms

[Fluent Forms](https://fluentforms.com/) offers many **Pre-built Form Templates**, so you do not have to build every form from scratch. This guide shows you how to create a form from a template, customize it, and publish it.

## Creating Forms using Pre-built Templates

Log in to your **WordPress Dashboard** and go to **Fluent Forms → Forms** in the left sidebar. Click **Add Form** at the top right of the **Forms** screen.

![Add new form](/images/creating-forms/form-builder-basics/using-and-customizing-pre-built-quick-forms/1.-Add-new-form-button-1-scaled.webp)

The **Create A New Form** window opens. Click **Choose a Template**.

![Choose template tab](/images/creating-forms/form-builder-basics/using-and-customizing-pre-built-quick-forms/Choose-a-template-pre-built.webp)

The **Choose a Template** screen opens. Pick a pre-made template to get started quickly. To build from scratch instead, see [How to Create a Form with Fluent Forms](/create-a-form).

The templates are organized into these categories:

- Basic
- Marketing
- Product
- Education
- Nonprofit
- IT
- Finance
- HR
- Social
- Government
- Healthcare

Some templates need Fluent Forms Pro. Those templates show the message "This template needs the Pro add-on of Fluent Forms."

Browse the category buttons at the top, or enter a name in the **Search a form template** box. Click the template you want. Fluent Forms creates the form and opens it in the editor.

![Form templates](/images/creating-forms/form-builder-basics/using-and-customizing-pre-built-quick-forms/pre-built-Form-Template.webp)

## Pre-built Form Customization

You can customize any field in the template.

Hover over a field and click the **Pencil Icon** in the field toolbar. The **Customize** tab opens on the right with the options for that field.

![Template editor](/images/creating-forms/form-builder-basics/using-and-customizing-pre-built-quick-forms/Editor-Fluentform-6-scaled.webp)

## Embedding Form into Frontend

Click **Save** in the top right corner (or press **Cmd/Ctrl + S**) to save your changes.

Click the **Preview** (eye) icon to preview your form.

Then click the copy icon next to the form shortcode in the top bar to copy it.

![Save form](/images/creating-forms/form-builder-basics/using-and-customizing-pre-built-quick-forms/Save-form-button-scaled.webp)

Paste the shortcode into the **Page** or **Post** where you want the form to appear.

![Embed shortcode](/images/creating-forms/form-builder-basics/using-and-customizing-pre-built-quick-forms/job-listing-form-scaled.webp)

This is the preview of the new form.

![Template form preview](/images/creating-forms/form-builder-basics/using-and-customizing-pre-built-quick-forms/Job-Listing-information-collect-form.webp)

Next, [style your form](/form-layout-settings) or [set up notifications](/notifications-confirmations-overview).
