# Create a Form with AI

Fluent Forms includes a built-in AI form builder. This guide shows you how to create a form with **Fluent Forms AI**, then customize it and publish it on your site.

> [!Note]
> **Fluent Forms AI** is the built-in, free AI assistant for quick form creation. It needs no extra setup. To generate forms with your own OpenAI API key, use **OpenAI ChatGPT** (Pro). See [How to Create a Form with OpenAI ChatGPT](/create-a-form-with-openai-chatgpt).

## Create Forms using Fluent Forms AI

Log in to your **WordPress Dashboard**, go to **Fluent Forms → Forms**, and click **Add Form** at the top right of the **Forms** screen.

![Add new form](/images/creating-forms/form-builder-basics/create-a-form-with-ai/1.-Add-new-form-button-scaled-2.webp)

The **Create A New Form** window opens. The **Create a Form Using AI** section sits below the form type options. You do not need to switch tabs.

![Create using AI tab](/images/creating-forms/form-builder-basics/create-a-form-with-ai/2.-Create-Using-AI-tab.webp)

This section has these options:

 * **Prompt box:** Describe the form you want to create (for example, a survey, payment, or registration form). The box is labeled **FluentForm AI creates it for you**.
 * **Suggestions:** Pick a ready-made prompt, such as **Newsletter Signup**, **Book Appointment**, **Customer Feedback**, **Event Signup**, **Membership Join**, **Product Review**, **Job Application**, **Donation Support**, or **Onboarding Checklist**. Fluent Forms adds the prompt to the box.
 * **Add specific questions:** Click this to add detail about the information you want in your form.

For example, to collect reviews for your products, choose the **Product Review** suggestion. You can also add your own instructions.

Click **Generate the Form**.

![Fluent Forms AI create](/images/creating-forms/form-builder-basics/create-a-form-with-ai/3.-Create-button.webp)

Fluent Forms AI generates the **Product Review Form**.

To rename the form, click the **Pencil Icon** next to the form name at the top left.

![AI form editor](/images/creating-forms/form-builder-basics/create-a-form-with-ai/4.-Form-is-ready-scaled.webp)

## Form Customization

On the **Editor** page of your new form, you can customize the generated fields.

Hover over a field to see these options:

* **Drag & Drop:** Move the field anywhere in the form.
* **Move-Up:** Move the field one step up.
* **Move-Down:** Move the field one step down.
* **Edit:** Click the **Pencil Icon** to open the **Customize** tab on the right, which has many customization options.
* **Duplicate:** Click this button to duplicate the field.
* **Delete:** Click the **Trash Icon** to delete the field.

![Input customization](/images/creating-forms/form-builder-basics/create-a-form-with-ai/5.-Input-Customization-scaled.webp)

## Add New Fields

You can also add new fields on the **Editor** page.

Go to the **Input Fields** tab on the right, open a group such as **General Fields**, and drag the field into your form.

You can also search for a field by name in the search box. Press the **/** key to jump to it.

> **Tip:** See the [Form Fields overview](/form-fields-overview) for guides on each input type.

![Add form fields](/images/creating-forms/form-builder-basics/create-a-form-with-ai/6.-Add-new-fields-scaled.webp)

## Embed Form into Frontend

Click the copy icon next to the **Shortcode** in the top bar, then paste the shortcode into the **Page** or **Post** where you want the form.

To preview the form, click the **Preview** (eye) icon.

Click **Save** (or press **Cmd/Ctrl + S**) to save your changes.

![Save Fluent Forms](/images/creating-forms/form-builder-basics/create-a-form-with-ai/7.-Save-Form-scaled.webp)
