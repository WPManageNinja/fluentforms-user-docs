# Create a Form with AI

Fluent Forms now integrates Fluent Forms AI, making it easier for users to create Forms effortlessly with simple commands. This article will guide you through creating a form using **Fluent Forms AI**.

> [!Note]
> **Fluent Forms AI** is the built-in, free AI assistant for quick form creation—no extra setup required. For advanced generation with your own OpenAI API key, use **OpenAI ChatGPT** (Pro). See [How to Create a Form with OpenAI ChatGPT](/create-a-form-with-openai-chatgpt).

## Create Forms using Fluent Forms AI

To start, log into your **WordPress Dashboard**. Now, navigate to **Fluent Forms > Forms** and click the **Add Form** button at the top right of the **Forms** screen.

![Add new form](/images/creating-forms/form-builder-basics/create-a-form-with-ai/1.-Add-new-form-button-scaled-2.webp)

The **Create A New Form** window opens. The **Create a Form Using AI** section sits below the form type options, so you do not need to switch tabs.

![Create using AI tab](/images/creating-forms/form-builder-basics/create-a-form-with-ai/2.-Create-Using-AI-tab.webp)

This section has the following options:

 * **Prompt box:** Describe the form you want to create (for example, a survey, payment, or registration form). The box is labeled **FluentForm AI creates it for you**.
 * **Suggestions:** Pick one of the ready-made prompts, such as **Newsletter Signup**, **Customer Feedback**, or **Product Review**. The prompt is added to the box automatically.
 * **Add specific questions:** Click this to add extra detail about the information you want in your form.

**For example**, to collect reviews for your products, choose the **Product Review** suggestion. You can also add instructions based on your requirements.

Once you are done, click the **Generate the Form** button.

![Fluent Forms AI create](/images/creating-forms/form-builder-basics/create-a-form-with-ai/3.-Create-button.webp)

Here you can see that the **Product Review Form** is fully ready with less time and effort only by using a few simple commands of Fluent Forms AI!

Also, you can rename the form by clicking the **Pencil Icon** next to the form name at the top left.

![AI form editor](/images/creating-forms/form-builder-basics/create-a-form-with-ai/4.-Form-is-ready-scaled.webp)

## Form Customization

Once you are on the Editor page of your newly created form you can customize the added fields according to your needs.

To customize an added field, hover over it and the following customization options will appear. 

a. **Drag & Drop**: With this option, you can move your fields anywhere easily.

b. **Move-Up**: Using this arrow, you can move your fields one step up.

c. **Move-Down**: Using this arrow, you can move your fields one step down.

d. **Edit**: Click the **Pencil Icon** to open the **Customize** tab on the right, which offers a wide range of options for customization.

e. **Duplicate**: Click this button and the field will be duplicated.

f. **Delete**: Click this **Trash Icon** and your field will be deleted.

![Input customization](/images/creating-forms/form-builder-basics/create-a-form-with-ai/5.-Input-Customization-scaled.webp)

## Add New Fields

From the **Editor** page of your newly created form, you can also add new fields based on your needs.

To add fields, go to the **Input Fields** tab on the right side, open the group you need, such as **General Fields**, and drag the field into your form.

You can also **search for a field** by name using the search box. Press the **/** key to jump to it.

> **Tip:** See the [Form Fields overview](/form-fields-overview) for guides on each input type.

![Add form fields](/images/creating-forms/form-builder-basics/create-a-form-with-ai/6.-Add-new-fields-scaled.webp)

## Embed Form into Frontend

Once you complete the process, you can embed and display the form on the desired Page/Post of your WordPress Site by clicking the copy icon next to the **Shortcode** in the top bar.

Also, to see the form preview, click the **Preview** (eye) icon.

Finally, press the **Save** button (or **Cmd/Ctrl + S**) to save all your changes.

![Save Fluent Forms](/images/creating-forms/form-builder-basics/create-a-form-with-ai/7.-Save-Form-scaled.webp)
