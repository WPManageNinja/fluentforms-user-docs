# Create a Form

[Fluent Forms](https://fluentforms.com/) is a drag-and-drop WordPress form builder. You can create contact forms, surveys, payment forms, and more without writing code.

This guide walks you through building a form from scratch. You add fields, customize settings, save your work, and embed the form on your site. New to the plugin? Read [Getting Started With Fluent Forms](/getting-started) and the Fluent Forms User Interface overview first.

You can also start from a [pre-built template](/using-and-customizing-pre-built-quick-forms), use [Fluent Forms AI](/create-a-form-with-ai), or build a [conversational form](/create-a-conversational-form) instead.

## Creating a New Form

Log in to your **WordPress Dashboard** and go to **Fluent Forms → Forms** in the left sidebar. Click **Add Form** at the top right of the **Forms** screen.

![Add new form](/images/creating-forms/form-builder-basics/create-a-form/1.-Add-new-form-button-scaled.webp)

The **Create A New Form** window opens with these options:

 * **New Blank Form:** Build a form from scratch.
 * **Conversational Form:** Turn content or surveys into interactive, one-question-at-a-time conversations.
 * **Choose a Template:** Pick a pre-made template from the categories instead of building from scratch. Premium templates need the Fluent Forms Pro Add-on installed and activated.
 * **Create a Post Form:** Build a form that creates WordPress posts.
 * **Create a Form Using AI:** Describe the form you need in the box below the options, or pick a suggestion, then click **Generate the Form**.

You can also **import** a ready-made form with **Import Form** at the top right of the window. See [How to Import and Export Fluent Forms](/import-and-export-fluent-forms) for details.

This example creates a form from scratch to show the whole process. Click **New Blank Form**.

![New form options](/images/creating-forms/form-builder-basics/create-a-form/Forms-Fluent-Forms-04-22-2025_03_23_PM-scaled.webp)

### Adding Fields

The **Editor** page opens. Click the **Pencil Icon** next to the form name at the top left to **rename** your form.

To add fields, use the **Input Fields** tab on the right. Open a field group such as **General Fields**, or search for a field by name in the search box. Press the **/** key to jump to the search box.

![Form editor](/images/creating-forms/form-builder-basics/create-a-form/3.-Editor-page-1-scaled.webp)

The **Pencil Icon** opens the **Rename Form** window. Type the new title and click **Save**.

![Rename form](/images/creating-forms/form-builder-basics/create-a-form/Edit-your-form-name.webp)

Add the fields you need. Click a **Field** button, or **drag and drop** it into the **Editor**. To learn more about adding fields, see the [Form Fields overview](/form-fields-overview).

![Drag drop fields](/images/creating-forms/form-builder-basics/create-a-form/5.-Drag-Drop-feature.gif)

### Customizing Form

To customize a field, hover over it and click the **Pencil Icon** in the field toolbar. You can also click the field and open the **Customize** tab on the right.

The **Customize** tab has many options, from renaming a field to setting the maximum size of a file input field.

![Field customization](/images/creating-forms/form-builder-basics/create-a-form/6.-Edit-Icon-or-Input-Customization-button-scaled.webp)

### Form edit history

Click the **History** (clock) icon at the top right of the right panel to open **Saved versions** and see recent changes to your form.

* **Restore:** Revert the form to that saved version.
* **Trash icon:** Clears all saved versions at once. You find it at the top of **Saved versions**.

![Form edit history](/images/creating-forms/form-builder-basics/create-a-form/7.-History-tab-scaled.webp)

## Embedding Form into Frontend

Click **Save** in the top right corner (or press **Cmd/Ctrl + S**) to save your changes. Click the **Preview** (eye) icon to preview your form.

Then click the copy icon next to the form shortcode in the top bar to copy it.

![Save and shortcode](/images/creating-forms/form-builder-basics/create-a-form/8.-Save-form-copy-shortcode-and-Preview-scaled.webp)

Paste the shortcode into the **Page** or **Post** where you want the form to appear.

![Paste form shortcode](/images/creating-forms/form-builder-basics/create-a-form/shortcode-paste.webp)
