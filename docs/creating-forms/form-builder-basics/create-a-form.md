# Create a Form

[Fluent Forms](https://fluentforms.com/) is a drag-and-drop WordPress form builder. You can create contact forms, surveys, payment forms, and more without writing code.

This guide walks you through building a form from scratch—adding fields, customizing settings, saving your work, and embedding the form on your site. New to the plugin? See [Getting Started With Fluent Forms](/getting-started) and the [Fluent Forms User Interface](/user-interface) overview first.

You can also start from a [pre-built template](/using-and-customizing-pre-built-quick-forms), use [Fluent Forms AI](/create-a-form-with-ai), or build a [conversational form](/create-a-conversational-form) instead.

## Creating a New Form

To start, log in to your **WordPress Dashboard**. Now, navigate to the **Fluent Forms > Forms** from the left sidebar. Then click the **Add Form** button at the top right of the **Forms** screen.

![Add new form](/images/creating-forms/form-builder-basics/create-a-form/1.-Add-new-form-button-scaled.webp)

The **Create A New Form** window opens with these options:

 * **New Blank Form:** Build a form from scratch.
 * **Conversational Form:** Turn content or surveys into interactive, one-question-at-a-time conversations.
 * **Choose a Template:** Pick a pre-made template, divided into categories, to avoid building a form from scratch. To use the premium templates, you need to install and activate the Fluent Forms Pro Add-on.
 * **Create a Post Form:** Build a form that creates WordPress posts.
 * **Create a Form Using AI:** Describe the form you need in the box below the options, or pick one of the suggestions, and click **Generate the Form**.

You can also **import** ready-made forms by clicking the **Import Form** button at the top right of the window. See [How to Import and Export Fluent Forms](/import-and-export-fluent-forms) for details. **For example**, here we create a **Blank Form** from scratch to show you the whole process of creating a new form.

First, click **New Blank Form**.

![New form options](/images/creating-forms/form-builder-basics/create-a-form/Forms-Fluent-Forms-04-22-2025_03_23_PM-scaled.webp)

### Adding Fields

The **Editor** page opens. Click the **Pencil Icon** next to the form name at the top left to **rename** your form.

To add fields, use the **Input Fields** tab on the right. Open a field group such as **General Fields**, or **search for a field** by name with the search box. Press the **/** key to jump to the search box quickly.

![Form editor](/images/creating-forms/form-builder-basics/create-a-form/3.-Editor-page-1-scaled.webp)

When you click the **Pencil Icon**, the **Rename Form** window opens. Type the new title and click the **Save** button.

![Rename form](/images/creating-forms/form-builder-basics/create-a-form/Edit-your-form-name.webp)

Now add fields according to your requirements by clicking the desired **Field** buttons or just **Drag and Drop** them into the **Editor.** To learn more about adding fields, see the [Form Fields overview](/form-fields-overview).

![Drag drop fields](/images/creating-forms/form-builder-basics/create-a-form/5.-Drag-Drop-feature.gif)

### Customizing Form

To customize a field, hover over it and click the **Pencil Icon** in the field toolbar. You can also click the field and open the **Customize** tab on the right.

The **Customize** tab offers a wide range of options, from renaming a field to setting the maximum size of a file input field.

![Field customization](/images/creating-forms/form-builder-basics/create-a-form/6.-Edit-Icon-or-Input-Customization-button-scaled.webp)

### Form edit history

Click the **History** (clock) icon at the top right of the right panel to open **Saved versions** and view recent changes to your form.

* **Restore:** Revert the form to a previous saved state.
* **Delete:** Remove a history entry you no longer need.

![Form edit history](/images/creating-forms/form-builder-basics/create-a-form/7.-History-tab-scaled.webp)

## Embedding Form into Frontend

After customization, click the **Save** button in the top right corner (or press **Cmd/Ctrl + S**) to save all the changes.
Click the **Preview** (eye) icon if you want to see the preview of your form.

Then, click the copy icon next to the form shortcode in the top bar to copy it and display the form on the front end.

![Save and shortcode](/images/creating-forms/form-builder-basics/create-a-form/8.-Save-form-copy-shortcode-and-Preview-scaled.webp)

Paste the shortcode in your desired **Page/Post** where you want this shortcode to appear on your WordPress Site.

![Paste form shortcode](/images/creating-forms/form-builder-basics/create-a-form/shortcode-paste.webp)
