# Creating Forms with Fluent Forms

Building a form should feel effortless — and with **Fluent Forms** it is. Whether you want a simple contact form, a guided multi-step experience, an interactive conversational flow, or a ready-to-use template, you can go from idea to a published, working form in minutes. This page walks you through every way to create a form so you can pick the approach that fits your goal.

## Form Builder Basics

Start here to learn the core building blocks. The drag-and-drop editor lets you assemble any form visually, and AI can do the heavy lifting when you want a head start.

- [Create a Form](/how-to-create-a-form-with-fluent-forms) — build and publish your first form with the drag-and-drop editor.
- [Pre-built Quick Forms](/using-and-customizing-pre-built-quick-forms-in-fluent-forms) — launch faster with ready-made templates you can customize.
- [Create with Fluent Forms AI](/how-to-create-a-form-with-fluent-forms-ai) — describe what you need and let AI generate the form for you.
- [Create with OpenAI ChatGPT](/how-to-create-a-form-with-openai-chatgpt) — connect your own OpenAI key to draft forms from a prompt.
