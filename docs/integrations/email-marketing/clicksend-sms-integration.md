# ClickSend SMS Integration

**ClickSend** lets you send an SMS in real time when a form is submitted. This guide shows you how to connect your **ClickSend** account to **[Fluent Forms Pro](/upgrade-to-pro-add-on)** and add a ClickSend feed to a form.

## Enable ClickSend Integration

In the WordPress left sidebar, go to **Fluent Forms** → **Integrations** and turn on the **ClickSend** module.

![Integrations E2 B9 Test E2 Wordpress](/images/integrations/email-marketing/clicksend-sms-integration/Integrations-_E2_80_B9-Fluent-Forms-Test-_E2_80_94-WordPress-6.png)

Once you enable **ClickSend**, Fluent Forms asks for the **Sender Number, Username, and API Key.**

![Global Settings E2 B9 Test E2 Wordpress](/images/integrations/email-marketing/clicksend-sms-integration/Global-Settings-_E2_80_B9-Fluent-Forms-Test-_E2_80_94-WordPress-1-5.png)

### Get Your ClickSend Credentials

1. Go to your **ClickSend Account Dashboard**.
2. Click the profile icon and open account settings, or browse the **[Account link](https://dashboard.clicksend.com/account/subaccounts)**.
3. Find your API key in your account.

If you don’t have a ClickSend SMS account, you can sign up [**here**](https://dashboard.clicksend.com/#/signup/). You can also access your [API key](https://dashboard.clicksend.com/#/account/subaccount) from your account.

![Clicksend Dashboard](/images/integrations/email-marketing/clicksend-sms-integration/ClickSend-Dashboard-1.png)

You need a **ClickSend number** to send SMS notifications, so [**purchase a number**](https://dashboard.clicksend.com/#/numbers/sms) from ClickSend.

Copy the **Sender** **Number**, **Username**, **and API Key**.

![Clicksend SMS Fluent Forms](/images/integrations/email-marketing/clicksend-sms-integration/4-ClickSend-SMS.png)

## Connect ClickSend with Fluent Forms

1. Return to the **ClickSend Settings** page. To open it, go to **Integrations** in the top bar and click **Manage** next to the module.
2. Paste the **Sender** **Number**, **Username**, **and API Key** you copied from ClickSend into the matching fields.
3. Click **Save**.

![Global Settings E2 B9 Test E2 Wordpress](/images/integrations/email-marketing/clicksend-sms-integration/Global-Settings-_E2_80_B9-Fluent-Forms-Test-_E2_80_94-WordPress-1-1-3.png)

## Integrate ClickSend Feed in Forms

1. Go to **Forms** in the **Fluent Forms Navbar**.
2. Select the form you want to connect to **ClickSend** and open the **Configuration** tab.
3. Go to **Configuration** → **Integrations & Add-ons** → **Integrations**.
4. Click **Add New Integration** and select the **ClickSend** SMS Notification feed from the drawer.

![Integrations Fluent Forms](/images/integrations/email-marketing/clicksend-sms-integration/Form-Integrations-Fluent-Forms-2.png)

Name the feed and select a service from the **Services** dropdown: **Single SMS**, **SMS Campaign**, **Create Subscriber Contact**, **Add Contact List**, or **Add Email Campaign**. The steps below use **Single SMS**.

![Clicksend SMS Fluent Forms](/images/integrations/email-marketing/clicksend-sms-integration/6-ClickSend-SMS.png)

Select a **Message Body** and **To** (the number that receives the SMS). You can also fill in optional fields such as Message, SMS Template, and SMS Schedule.

Turn on **Conditional Logic** if you need it. See [Fluent Forms Conditional Logic](/set-up-forms-with-conditional-logic). Click **Save** when the configuration is done.

![Clicksend SMS Fluent Forms](/images/integrations/email-marketing/clicksend-sms-integration/7-ClickSend-SMS.png)
